We never sell, rent, or trade personal data or business contact lists to third parties for unsolicited marketing.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We only share necessary technical data with essential infrastructure providers (such as cloud hosting, database/storage services, map/navigation providers, and push notification services) strictly bound by confidentiality and security obligations.
