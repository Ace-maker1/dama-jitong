1. Support Overview (English)

Welcome to the official support center for the DamaJitong mobile application (iOS & Android) and website (damajitong.com). We respond to all inquiries within 24–48 business hours.

  • Claim or Update Business Listings: Send your business name, address, and updated details to our support email. Directory updates are completely free.
  • Account & Data Deletion (PDPA): Email us using your registered email address to request permanent deletion of your account or uploaded content. Verified requests are completed within 7 business days.
  • App Technical Issues: Please ensure you are using the latest app version and grant location permissions for map navigation.
